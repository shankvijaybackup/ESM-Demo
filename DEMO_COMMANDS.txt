SCENARIO 1: LEAVE APPLICATION (AUTO-APPROVED)
=================================================================

Natural Language Request:
"Apply for 2 days annual leave from Feb 20 to Feb 21"

curl -X POST http://localhost:3000/api/webhook/nlp \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "text": "Apply for 2 days annual leave from 2026-02-20 to 2026-02-21",
    "employeeId": "EMP001"
  }'

Expected Result: Auto-approved (≤ 2 days policy)
